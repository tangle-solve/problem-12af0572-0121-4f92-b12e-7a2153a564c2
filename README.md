### CLIM-000176: Copper and tin removal from steel scrap
- **Subfield:** Industrial heat, cement, and metals
- **Open:** Remove tramp copper and tin economically enough to produce demanding grades without routine dilution with primary iron.
- **Status:** Daehn et al. quantified the copper constraint in 2017; sorting and specialized refining provide partial solutions.
- **Type:** engineering
- **Why:** Enables repeated high-quality steel recycling.
